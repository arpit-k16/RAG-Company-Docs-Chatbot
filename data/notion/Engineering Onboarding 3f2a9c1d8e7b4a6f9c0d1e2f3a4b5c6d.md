# Engineering Onboarding

Welcome to Acme Engineering! This page is maintained in Notion by the Platform team.

## Week 1 checklist

- Get access to GitHub (org: acme-eng), Jira, and the #eng-general Slack channel.
- Set up your dev environment with `make bootstrap` in the `platform-tools` repo.
- Pair with your onboarding buddy on a "good first issue" ticket.
- Ship your first pull request to production by the end of week 1.

## Engineering practices

- Every pull request needs **at least one approving review** and green CI before merge.
- We deploy to production **multiple times a day** through the deploy pipeline; Friday deploys after
  15:00 IST need approval from the on-call lead.
- Incidents are tracked in the #incidents channel. Severity 1 incidents page the on-call engineer.

## On-call

Engineers join the on-call rotation after **3 months**. A rotation lasts one week. On-call engineers
receive an allowance of ₹5,000 per week of on-call.
