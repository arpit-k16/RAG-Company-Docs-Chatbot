# Acme Corp Company Handbook

_Version 4.0 — effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Operations._

## 1. About Acme Corp

Acme Corp builds workflow software for mid-sized manufacturers. We have offices in Bengaluru,
Pune and Singapore, and roughly 420 employees. Our values are **Customer First**, **Own the Outcome**
and **Default to Transparency**.

## 2. Working hours

Core collaboration hours are **11:00 to 16:00 IST**, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees
may plan their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled
outside core hours without the attendees' agreement.

## 3. Hybrid work

Employees work from the office **at least 2 days per week** (Tuesday plus one day agreed with their
manager). Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and HR. Employees can
claim a one-time **home-office allowance of ₹15,000** in their first 90 days.

## 4. Payroll

Salaries are credited on the **last working day of every month**. Payslips are available in the
HRMS portal (hr.acme.internal) under _My Pay_. Payroll queries go to payroll@acme.example.

## 5. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in **April** (full review with compensation changes) and
**October** (mid-year check-in, no compensation changes). Employees write a self-review, collect
feedback from 2–4 peers, and meet their manager for a calibration conversation.

## 6. Learning budget

Each employee has a **learning budget of ₹40,000 per financial year** for courses, certifications,
books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Submit requests through the HRMS portal
under _Learning_ with manager approval.

## 7. Travel and expenses

- Domestic flights: economy class, booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance.
- Hotels: up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities, ₹4,000 elsewhere.
- Daily meal allowance: ₹1,200 domestic, USD 50 international.
- Expense claims must be filed within **30 days** with receipts, via the Expenses app.

## 8. Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and
discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR, or
anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics.acme.internal.
